Upcoming Buddhist Festivals

Losar Festival

February / March

Marking the Tibetan New Year, Losar is a vibrant festival celebrated with immense fervor, symbolizing a farewell to the old year and a welcoming of the new with hope and purity.

Saga Dawa

May / June

This is the most sacred Buddhist festival, commemorating the birth, enlightenment, and passing (parinirvana) of Lord Buddha through grand processions and acts of kindness.

Drupka Tsheshi

July / August

This festival celebrates the Buddha's first sermon on the Four Noble Truths at the deer park in Sarnath. It is observed with prayers and yak races at the revered Muguthang monastery.

Lhabab Duchen

October / November

One of the four great Buddhist festivals, it marks the anniversary of the Buddha's descent from the Heaven of Thirty-Three where he had been teaching the Dharma to his mother.

Plan Your Visit

Best Time to Visit

Spring (March to May): Pleasant weather with clear views of the Himalayas. Rhododendrons are in full bloom, making it a picturesque time to travel.

Autumn (October to December): Crisp, clear skies after the monsoon, offering the best mountain views. Ideal for trekking and photography.

Monsoon season (June to September): Generally avoided due to heavy rainfall and potential landslides.

Getting There Safely

By Air: The nearest major airport is Bagdogra (IXB) in West Bengal. From there, it's a 4-5 hour scenic drive to Gangtok. Pakyong Airport (PYG) in Sikkim has limited connectivity.

By Road: Roads from Siliguri/Bagdogra are the primary entry point. Always travel during the day and hire experienced local drivers who are familiar with mountain terrain. Check road conditions before travel, especially during monsoon.

Permits: An Inner Line Permit (ILP) is required for most international tourists. Ensure you have all necessary documents ready.
